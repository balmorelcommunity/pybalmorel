pybalmorel.backcasting.nuclear

Nuclear Availability

Turn ENTSO-E’s observed nuclear output into weekly GKRATE overrides (GKRATE_ENTSOE.inc) for a Backcast: per country and Backcast Calendar week, the highest hourly nuclear output divided by the country’s nuclear GKFX, clipped at 1 (see CONTEXT.md). Reuses the override-file pattern of docs/adr/0006; the scenario’s GKRATE.inc includes the file itself.

Created on 22.10.2026 @author: Mathias Berg Rosendal

PostDoc at DTU Management (Energy Economics & Modelling)

pybalmorel.backcasting.nuclear.nuclear_capacity(gkfx: pandas.DataFrame, gdata: pandas.DataFrame, fdata: pandas.DataFrame, rrraaa: pandas.DataFrame, year: int) → pandas.DataFrame

The nuclear GKFX of year per area and technology, with its canonical region.

Nuclear technologies are those whose GDATA(G,’GDFUEL’) equals FDATA(‘NUCLEAR’,’FDACRONYM’). Both are GAMS acronyms that get_input returns as the same large float, so the two values are compared rather than hard-coded. Columns are taken by position, as get_input may name them after the raw GAMS domains.

Args:

gkfx (pd.DataFrame): GKFX from get_input, ending in (Y, AAA, G, Value). gdata (pd.DataFrame): GDATA from get_input, ending in (G, GDATASET, Value). fdata (pd.DataFrame): FDATA from get_input, ending in (FFF, FDATASET, Value). rrraaa (pd.DataFrame): RRRAAA from get_input, ending in (RRR, AAA). year (int): The Backcast Year, the Y of GKFX.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Columns ‘Area’, ‘Technology’, ‘Region’ (canonical) and

‘Capacity’ (MW), for every nuclear (area, technology) with GKFX > 0.

pybalmorel.backcasting.nuclear.nuclear_ratings(observed: dict[str, pandas.Series], capacity: pandas.DataFrame, year: int) → pandas.DataFrame

The weekly Nuclear Availability per canonical region.

The rating of week s is the highest hourly nuclear output in that week of the Backcast Calendar divided by the region’s summed nuclear GKFX. Missing hours are ignored. Weeks without any data after the region’s last week with data are a closure and get 0, since ENTSO-E stops reporting a closed fleet; any other week without data gets NaN. Ratings above 1 are clipped. All of these are reported.

Args:
observed (dict[str, pd.Series]): Hourly nuclear output (MW) per

canonical region, indexed by timezone-aware timestamps.

capacity (pd.DataFrame): Output of nuclear_capacity. year (int): The Backcast Year.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Index ‘S01’..’S52’, one column per region that has both

nuclear GKFX and observed nuclear output.
