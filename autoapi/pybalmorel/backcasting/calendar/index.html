pybalmorel.backcasting.calendar

Backcast Calendar

The single mapping between a Backcast Year’s clock time and Balmorel’s 52 seasons (S01-S52) x 168 terms (T001-T168), see CONTEXT.md and docs/adr/0008. S01 T001 is 00:00 UTC on the first Monday of the year, every day has exactly 24 terms, and the days outside the 52 full weeks are dropped.

Every conversion between timestamps and (Season, Time) labels goes through this module - nowhere else should build its own hourly index or assign by position.

pybalmorel.backcasting.calendar.from_backcast_grid(df: pandas.DataFrame, year: int, season_col: str = 'Season', term_col: str = 'Time') → pandas.DataFrame

Replace (Season, Term) label columns with a UTC ‘Time’ timestamp column.

Rows are matched by label, never by position, so missing or reordered (S,T) rows can’t shift the rest of the year.

Args:
df (pd.DataFrame): Long-format data with season and term label columns,

e.g. a MainResults symbol with ‘Season’ and ‘Time’.

year (int): The Backcast Year. season_col (str, optional): Name of the season column. Defaults to ‘Season’. term_col (str, optional): Name of the term column. Defaults to ‘Time’.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: df without the label columns and with a ‘Time’ column

of UTC timestamps first.

pybalmorel.backcasting.calendar.to_backcast_grid(hourly: pandas.Series, year: int) → pandas.DataFrame

Reshape an hourly, timezone-aware series onto the 52 x 168 (S,T) grid.

Args:
hourly (pd.Series): Values indexed by timezone-aware hourly timestamps.

Converted to UTC before matching; hours outside the Backcast Calendar are dropped.

year (int): The Backcast Year.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Index ‘S01’..’S52’, columns ‘T001’..’T168’. Hours

missing from hourly are NaN.

pybalmorel.backcasting.calendar.daily_to_backcast_grid(daily: pandas.Series, year: int) → pandas.DataFrame

Map a daily series onto the (S,T) grid, each value covering its UTC day.

Days missing from daily (weekends/holidays without trading data) carry the last available value forward, or the first one backward for gaps before the first observation.

Args:

daily (pd.Series): Values indexed by calendar date (timezone-naive). year (int): The Backcast Year.

Returns:

pd.DataFrame: Index ‘S01’..’S52’, columns ‘T001’..’T168’.
