pybalmorel.backcasting.vre

VRE Level

Scale the weather year’s wind and solar full-load hours per canonical region and kind (solar, onshore, offshore) so that the output Balmorel can produce in the Backcast Year equals ENTSO-E’s reported output (see CONTEXT.md and ADR 0012). The hourly profiles and the spread between a region’s areas stay as the weather year wrote them. Writes WNDFLH_ENTSOE.inc and SOLEFLH_ENTSOE.inc and appends their $include to the scenario’s WNDFLH.inc and SOLEFLH.inc.

pybalmorel.backcasting.vre.available_vre(gkfx: pandas.DataFrame, gdata: pandas.DataFrame, rrraaa: pandas.DataFrame, wndflh: pandas.DataFrame, soleflh: pandas.DataFrame, gkrate: pandas.DataFrame, names: dict[float, str], year: int) → pandas.DataFrame

Balmorel’s wind and solar PV capacity of year per area and technology, with its available output.

Wind is every technology with fuel WIND; solar PV every technology of type GSOLE with fuel SUN, so solar heat isn’t counted. Available output is GKFX x FLH x the mean weekly GKRATE (1 where unset). An area with GKFX and no full-load hours warns and counts for nothing.

Args:

gkfx (pd.DataFrame): GKFX from get_input, ending in (Y, AAA, G, Value). gdata (pd.DataFrame): GDATA from get_input, ending in (G, GDATASET, Value). rrraaa (pd.DataFrame): RRRAAA from get_input, ending in (RRR, AAA). wndflh (pd.DataFrame): WNDFLH from get_input, ending in (AAA, Value). soleflh (pd.DataFrame): SOLEFLH from get_input, ending in (AAA, Value). gkrate (pd.DataFrame): GKRATE from get_input, ending in (AAA, G, SSS, Value). names (dict[float, str]): Output of thermal.acronym_names. year (int): The Backcast Year, the Y of GKFX.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Columns ‘Area’, ‘Technology’, ‘Region’, ‘Kind’,

‘Capacity’ (MW), ‘FLH’, ‘Rating’ and ‘Available’ (TWh).

pybalmorel.backcasting.vre.vre_factors(available: pandas.DataFrame, observed: pandas.DataFrame, bounds: tuple[float, float] = FACTOR_BOUNDS) → pandas.DataFrame

The factor per canonical region and kind that makes Balmorel’s available output ENTSO-E’s.

Where ENTSO-E reports no offshore output for a region with Balmorel offshore capacity, onshore and offshore share one factor (kind ‘Wind’), since ENTSO-E books small offshore farms as onshore. A factor outside bounds, or of a region without ENTSO-E generation data, is not written and warns.

Args:

available (pd.DataFrame): Output of available_vre. observed (pd.DataFrame): Output of observed_vre. bounds (tuple[float, float], optional): Lowest and highest factor

written. Defaults to FACTOR_BOUNDS.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Columns ‘Region’, ‘Kind’, ‘GKFX’ (MW), ‘AvailableTWh’,

‘ObservedTWh’, ‘Factor’ and ‘Written’, one row per region and kind with Balmorel capacity.

pybalmorel.backcasting.vre.write_flh_override(scaled: pandas.DataFrame, factors: pandas.DataFrame, symbol: str, year: int, scenario_data_path: str | pathlib.Path) → pybalmorel.classes.IncFile

Write <symbol>_ENTSOE.inc with the scaled full-load hours of one symbol.

The file is written even without lines, since the scenario’s include falls back to base’s file where it is missing. The header lists the observed output each region and kind is scaled to, not the factor, so a rerun on the scaled input data writes the same file.

Args:

scaled (pd.DataFrame): Output of scaled_flh. factors (pd.DataFrame): Output of vre_factors. symbol (str): ‘WNDFLH’ or ‘SOLEFLH’. year (int): The Backcast Year. scenario_data_path (str | Path): The scenario’s ‘data’ folder.

Returns:

IncFile: The saved IncFile instance.
