pybalmorel.backcasting.co2tax

CO2 Tax

Convert EEX EUA primary-auction results (Balmorel’s backcast/historicalprices/) into Balmorel’s season/term-resolved TAX_CO2_ST(YYY,CCCRRRAAA,SSS,TTT) input (see the Balmorel repo’s docs/adr/0002-co2-tax-season-term-resolution.md).

Every country gets the EUA price unless overridden (e.g. the UK’s own carbon price, or 0 for countries without one). Prices are expressed in the backcast Price Year (see pricelevel.py) and mapped onto the UTC Backcast Calendar (see calendar.py).

pybalmorel.backcasting.co2tax.load_eex_auction_prices(xlsx_path: str | pathlib.Path) → pandas.Series

Load daily EUA primary-auction clearing prices in nominal EUR/tCO2.

Only successful auctions are used. Days with several auctions (e.g. the EU, DE and PL auctions) get the mean of their clearing prices.

Args:
xlsx_path (str | Path): An EEX ‘emission-spot-primary-market-auction-report’

file (header in the sixth row, columns ‘Date’, ‘Status’ and ‘Auction Price €/tCO2’).

Returns:

pd.Series: Daily nominal price in EUR/tCO2, indexed by calendar date.
