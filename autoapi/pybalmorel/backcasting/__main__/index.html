Join hourly generation per category on the full grid of the Backcast Calendar.

MainResults leaves out zeros, so a category Balmorel has no row for in an hour produced 0. ENTSO-E’s missing categories are 0 too, but only in hours where ENTSO-E reports some generation for the region; a region-hour without any ENTSO-E generation is left out on both sides, and so are the regions in INCOMPLETE_GENERATION.

Args:

pd.DataFrame: Index (Region, Technology, Time), columns ‘ENTSOE’ and ‘BALMOREL’.

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.nuclear_availability(balmorel_scenario_path, entsoe_data_path, year, reload_input)

Write GKRATE_ENTSOE.inc: weekly Nuclear Availability from ENTSO-E nuclear output.

Per country and Backcast Calendar week: the highest hourly nuclear output in the generation csv’s under ENTSOE_DATA_PATH (from download –query generation), divided by the country’s nuclear GKFX in YEAR, clipped at 1. pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.thermal_availability(balmorel_scenario_path, entsoe_data_path, year, reload_input)

Write GKRATE_THERMAL_ENTSOE.inc: weekly Thermal Availability from ENTSO-E capacity and outages.

Per country, fuel group and Backcast Calendar week: ENTSO-E’s installed capacity in YEAR as a share of the group’s GKFX (at most 1), less the share reported out of service that week (from download –query installed_generation_capacity –query unavailability_of_generation_units). Writes <balmorel-scenario-path>/data/GKRATE_THERMAL_ENTSOE.inc, which the scenario’s GKRATE.inc must include, and backcastoutput/thermal_capacity.csv, which checks GKFX against installed capacity and observed output. See CONTEXT.md.

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.must_run_floor(balmorel_scenario_path, entsoe_data_path, year, price_threshold, min_hours, every_week, reload_input)

Write the MUSTRUN files: weekly must-run floors from ENTSO-E output in low-price weeks.

Per country, fuel group and Backcast Calendar week: the lowest hourly output in the generation csv’s under ENTSOE_DATA_PATH, for weeks with at least MIN_HOURS day-ahead prices at or below PRICE_THRESHOLD, and for the –every-week groups in all weeks. Capped at Balmorel’s available capacity, so run thermal-availability first. Writes MUSTRUN_ZONE, MUSTRUN_ZONE_RRR, MUSTRUN_GROUP, MUSTRUN_GROUP_FFF and, last, MUSTRUN_FLOOR (.inc) to <balmorel-scenario-path>/data/; MUSTRUN_FLOOR.inc switches Balmorel’s MUSTRUN addon on. See CONTEXT.md.

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.demand_profile(balmorel_scenario_path, entsoe_data_path, year, reload_input)

Write DE_VAR_T_ENTSOE.inc: electricity demand profiles from ENTSO-E actual load.

Per Balmorel region with electricity demand: the hourly ENTSO-E load of its canonical region in YEAR (from download –query load) on the Backcast Calendar, as DE_VAR_T for the users OTHER and RESE. Implausible hours are interpolated. Writes <balmorel-scenario-path>/data/DE_VAR_T_ENTSOE.inc and appends its $include to the scenario’s DE_VAR_T.inc. Rerun after writing new weather-year data, which overwrites DE_VAR_T.inc. See CONTEXT.md.

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.demand_level(balmorel_scenario_path, entsoe_data_path, year, basis, coverage_bounds, eurostat_data_path, reload_input, heat, load_results)

Write DE_ENTSOE.inc: the annual electricity demand level from ENTSO-E load.

Per region: ENTSO-E’s load in YEAR (from download –query load) less what the previous backcast run added on top of DE: its transmission losses, the electricity it used for heat (ENDOGENOUS_ELECT2HEAT), and its distribution losses on anything but exogenous demand (on that heating, and on wind and solar output); all net of Balmorel’s distribution losses. That heating responds to prices, so solve, format, and run this again for as long as the printed residual keeps falling. With –heat eurostat (the first run of a new backcast year), Eurostat’s electricity for household space and water heating and for district heating is left out instead. Split over regions and users by the input data’s DE. Eurostat responses are fetched once and cached. Writes <balmorel-scenario-path>/data/DE_ENTSOE.inc, which the scenario’s DE.inc, INDUSTRY_DE.inc and INDIVUSERS_DE.inc must include, and backcastoutput/demand_level.csv. See CONTEXT.md and docs/adr/0015.

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.vre_flh(balmorel_scenario_path, entsoe_data_path, year, bounds, reload_input)

Write WNDFLH_ENTSOE.inc and SOLEFLH_ENTSOE.inc: VRE full-load hours scaled to ENTSO-E output.

Per country and kind (solar, onshore, offshore): one factor that makes Balmorel’s available output in YEAR (GKFX x FLH x GKRATE) equal ENTSO-E’s reported output (from download –query generation), applied to the full-load hours of every area of that country and kind. Writes both files to <balmorel-scenario-path>/data/, appends their $include to the scenario’s WNDFLH.inc and SOLEFLH.inc, and writes backcastoutput/vre_flh.csv. Rerun after every weather-year run, which overwrites WNDFLH.inc and SOLEFLH.inc. See CONTEXT.md.

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.download(entsoe_data_path, year, queries)

Download ENTSO-E data for YEAR into ENTSOE_DATA_PATH, for format and transmission-flow-limit.

Fetches every zone in pybalmorel.entsoe.bidding_zones, and the flows of every neighbouring pair of them. IE’s load is fetched for Ireland’s control area instead of the all-island IE_SEM zone. Files already downloaded, and pairs ENTSO-E had no data for on an earlier run, are skipped, so rerun to retry failed requests.

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.ttf_csv(html_path, historicalprices_path)

Split a saved Yahoo Finance TTF=F history page into natural_gas_TTF_<year>.csv.

HTML_PATH is the page https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TTF%3DF/history/ saved from a browser, covering any number of years. One CSV per year is written to HISTORICALPRICES_PATH in the format the fuelprices command reads.

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.fuelprices(balmorel_scenario_path, historicalprices_path, year)

Write FUELPRICE.inc + FUELPRICE_CONSTANT.inc: (S,T)-resolved historical NATGAS/COAL prices for backcasting.

Reads HISTORICALPRICES_PATH’s CSVs, converts them to the Price Year (EUR2016, bundled HICP and EUR/USD tables), maps them onto the UTC Backcast Calendar and writes <balmorel-scenario-path>/data/FUELPRICE.inc and FUELPRICE_CONSTANT.inc. See docs/adr/0001-fuelprice-season-term-resolution.md in the Balmorel repo. Warns if balopt.opt doesn’t set FUELPRICE_DOL=YYY_AAA_FFF_SSS_TTT (it is never edited).

pybalmorel.backcasting.__main__.co2_tax(balmorel_scenario_path, historicalprices_path, year, co2_overrides)

Write TAX_CO2_ST.inc: (S,T)-resolved historical CO2 tax for backcasting.

Reads the EEX EUA primary-auction report for YEAR under HISTORICALPRICES_PATH, converts it to the Price Year (EUR2016), maps it onto the UTC Backcast Calendar and writes <balmorel-scenario-path>/data/TAX_CO2_ST.inc. See docs/adr/0002-co2-tax-season-term-resolution.md in the Balmorel repo. Warns if balopt.opt doesn’t set TAX_CO2_DOL=YYY_CCC_SSS_TTT (it is never edited).
