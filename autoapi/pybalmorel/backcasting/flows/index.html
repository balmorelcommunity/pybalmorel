Flows

pybalmorel.backcasting.flows.compute_entsoe_transmission_overrides(balmorel_model_folder: str | pathlib.Path, scenario: str, year: int, entsoe_data_path: str | pathlib.Path, api_key: str, gams_system_directory: str | None = None) → tuple[pandas.DataFrame, list[tuple[str, str]]]

Compute Observed Max Flow XKFX overrides for a Balmorel scenario’s borders.

Borders are read from the scenario’s current XKFX (see get_current_borders). Internal links that ENTSO-E can’t distinguish (both sides sharing a canonical name, e.g. DE4-N <-> DE4-W) are left out untouched, per docs/adr/0006. Where several ENTSO-E zones collapse to one Balmorel region, their observed flows are summed; where several Balmorel regions collapse to one ENTSO-E zone, the observed flow is split evenly across them.

Args:

balmorel_model_folder (str | Path): Top-level Balmorel folder (contains ‘base’). scenario (str): The scenario to read/derive borders for. year (int): The backcast target year. entsoe_data_path (str | Path): Folder to fetch/read ENTSO-E csv’s from. api_key (str): ENTSO-E transparency platform API key. gams_system_directory (str | None, optional): GAMS system directory.

Returns:
tuple[pd.DataFrame, list[tuple[str, str]]]: Overrides (Year, RegionFrom,

RegionTo, Value) and the ENTSO-E zone pairs that could not be fetched.

pybalmorel.backcasting.flows.load_entsoe_flows(entsoe_data_path: str | pathlib.Path, year: int) → pandas.DataFrame

Hourly ENTSO-E physical flows per directed canonical border.

Each file is resampled to hourly means (MW, i.e. MWh per hour), then summed over every ENTSO-E zone pair that collapses to the same canonical border (e.g. the Italian sub-zones). Internal links, where both sides share a canonical name, are dropped. A pair fetched under several zone spellings (e.g. ‘DK_1’ and ‘10YDK-1——–W’) is read once.

Args:

entsoe_data_path (str | Path): Folder with the fetched crossborder_flows csv’s. year (int): The Backcast Year.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Columns ‘From’, ‘To’, ‘Time’ (UTC) and ‘Value’, restricted

to the Backcast Calendar.

pybalmorel.backcasting.flows.align_flows(entsoe_flows: pandas.DataFrame, balmorel_flows: pandas.DataFrame, limits: pandas.Series | None = None) → pandas.DataFrame

Join ENTSO-E and Balmorel flows on every border both datasets know.

A border counts as known to Balmorel if it appears in either direction in its results. Hours Balmorel leaves out of the GDX are zero flow, so they are filled with 0 rather than dropped - dropping them would bias every statistic towards the hours Balmorel used the border.

Args:

entsoe_flows (pd.DataFrame): Output of load_entsoe_flows. balmorel_flows (pd.DataFrame): Output of format_balmorel_flows. limits (pd.Series | None, optional): Observed Max Flow per border (see

entsoe_flow_limits), added as a ‘Limit’ column. Defaults to None.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Index (‘From’, ‘To’, ‘Time’), columns ‘ENTSOE’, ‘BALMOREL’

and, if limits are given, ‘Limit’.

pybalmorel.backcasting.flows.flow_statistics(flows: pandas.DataFrame, tolerance: float = 0.01) → pandas.DataFrame
