pybalmorel.backcasting.fuelprices

Fuel Prices

Convert collected historical fuel price series (Balmorel’s backcast/historicalprices/) into Balmorel’s season/term-resolved FUELPRICE(YYY,AAA,FFF,SSS,TTT) input (see the Balmorel repo’s docs/adr/0001-fuelprice-season-term-resolution.md).

Only NATGAS and COAL are converted here - CO2 belongs to TAX_CO2_ST (see co2tax.py), and oil is not covered yet. Prices are expressed in the backcast Price Year (see pricelevel.py) and mapped onto the UTC Backcast Calendar (see calendar.py).

Created on 13.08.2026 @author: Mathias Berg Rosendal

pybalmorel.backcasting.fuelprices.split_yahoo_ttf_html(html_path: str | pathlib.Path, output_dir: str | pathlib.Path) → list[pathlib.Path]

Split a saved Yahoo Finance TTF=F history page into natural_gas_TTF_<year>.csv files.

Yahoo Finance only offers its CSV download to paying users, but a saved copy of https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TTF%3DF/history/ (any date range, e.g. several years) holds the same table. Each calendar year in it is written as one CSV in the format load_ttf_gas_prices reads: ‘Date’ without the year (e.g. ‘Dec 30’) and Volume without thousands separators.

Args:

html_path (str | Path): The saved Yahoo Finance history page (.html). output_dir (str | Path): Folder to write natural_gas_TTF_<year>.csv to,

typically Balmorel’s backcast/historicalprices/. Existing files are overwritten.

Returns:

list[Path]: The written CSV files, one per year.

pybalmorel.backcasting.fuelprices.write_fuelprice_override(fuel_grids: dict[str, pandas.DataFrame], year: int, scenario_data_path: str | pathlib.Path) → pybalmorel.classes.IncFile

Write FUELPRICE.inc with (S,T)-resolved historical fuel prices.

Written as scenario-local ‘data/FUELPRICE.inc’, which bb4datainc.inc reads ahead of base/data/FUELPRICE.inc (and that fallback’s own separate FUELPRICE_%SCNAME%.inc indirection) - see docs/adr/0001 in the Balmorel repo. Requires FUELPRICE_DOL=YYY_AAA_FFF_SSS_TTT to be set in the scenario’s balopt.opt (not done here), and FUELPRICE_CONSTANT.inc (see write_fuelprice_constant_override) to list every other fuel, or the model densely populates IFUELPRICE for every fuel in FFF.

Args:
fuel_grids (dict[str, pd.DataFrame]): {FFF fuel name: (S,T) grid},

e.g. {‘NATGAS’: …, ‘COAL’: …} as returned by daily_to_backcast_grid.

year (int): The backcast year these prices apply to. scenario_data_path (str | Path): The scenario’s ‘data’ folder.

Returns:

IncFile: The saved IncFile instance.

pybalmorel.backcasting.fuelprices.write_fuelprice_constant_override(resolved_fuels: list[str], scenario_data_path: str | pathlib.Path) → pybalmorel.classes.IncFile

Write FUELPRICE_CONSTANT.inc flagging every fuel except resolved_fuels as annual-only.

Keeps IFUELPRICE’s dense (S,T) broadcast limited to the fuels that actually have historical daily data - required for FUELPRICE_DOL=YYY_AAA_FFF_SSS_TTT to be affordable (see docs/adr/0001, ‘FUELPRICE_CONSTANT’ section).

Args:
resolved_fuels (list[str]): FFF fuel names with real (S,T) data, e.g.

[‘NATGAS’, ‘COAL’] - excluded from the constant set.

scenario_data_path (str | Path): The scenario’s ‘data’ folder.

Returns:

IncFile: The saved IncFile instance.

pybalmorel.backcasting.fuelprices.generate_fuelprice_overrides(historicalprices_path: str | pathlib.Path, year: int, balmorel_scenario_path: str | pathlib.Path) → None

Convert backcast/historicalprices/ into FUELPRICE.inc + FUELPRICE_CONSTANT.inc.

Only NATGAS and COAL get real (S,T)-resolved prices - CO2 belongs to TAX_CO2_ST (see co2tax.py), and oil is not covered yet. Every other fuel is flagged constant (see docs/adr/0001 in the Balmorel repo). Prices are converted to the Price Year with the bundled HICP and EUR/USD tables.

Warns (without changing anything) if the scenario’s balopt.opt doesn’t set FUELPRICE_DOL=YYY_AAA_FFF_SSS_TTT, or if HYDROGEN_FUELPRICE.inc hardcodes NATGAS/COAL prices - in both cases Balmorel would ignore this override.

Args:

historicalprices_path (str | Path): Path to backcast/historicalprices/. year (int): The Backcast Year. balmorel_scenario_path (str | Path): The scenario folder (e.g. …/backcast).
