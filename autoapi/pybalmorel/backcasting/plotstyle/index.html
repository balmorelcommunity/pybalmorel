pybalmorel.backcasting.plotstyle

Plot Style

Colours and themes of the backcast validation plots: the ENTSO-E and Balmorel line colours, the technology category colours, and a light and a dark theme. The dark theme uses Catppuccin Mocha colours on a transparent background, so the plots sit on the background of a dark slide.

pybalmorel.backcasting.plotstyle.plot_style(style: str = 'light') → collections.abc.Generator[tuple[dict[str, str], dict[str, str]], None, None]

Theme the plots drawn inside the context.

‘light’ keeps matplotlib’s defaults, so the plots look as they did before the themes. ‘dark’ starts from matplotlib’s dark_background, colours text, ticks, spines, grid and legends with the dark tokens, and makes the figure and axes backgrounds transparent. Save a dark figure with transparent=True.

Args:

style (str, optional): ‘light’ or ‘dark’. Defaults to ‘light’.

Raises:

ValueError: If style is neither ‘light’ nor ‘dark’.

Yields:
tuple[dict[str, str], dict[str, str]]: The theme’s colour tokens, and

the category colours with DARK_COLOR_OVERRIDES applied in ‘dark’.
