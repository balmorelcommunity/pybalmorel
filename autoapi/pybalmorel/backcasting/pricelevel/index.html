pybalmorel.backcasting.pricelevel

Price Level

Convert nominal historical prices into the backcast Price Year (€2016, see CONTEXT.md and docs/adr/0009): deflate with the euro-area HICP and convert USD with the Eurostat annual-average exchange rate.

The tables are bundled rather than fetched, so backcast inputs are reproducible offline. Extend them once a year from: - HICP: Eurostat prc_hicp_aind, geo=EA (changing composition), coicop=CP00,

unit=INX_A_AVG (annual average index, 2015=100)

  • EUR/USD: Eurostat ert_bil_eur_a, currency=USD, statinfo=AVG (USD per EUR)
