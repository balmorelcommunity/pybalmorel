pybalmorel.backcasting.eurostat

Eurostat Energy Balances

Fetch the Eurostat statistics the Demand Level needs from the public dissemination API (no key): final electricity consumption and electric boilers and heat pumps in district heating (nrg_bal_c), and households’ electricity for space and water heating (nrg_d_hhq). Responses are JSON-stat and cached as <eurostat-data-path>/<year>_<dataset>.json, so a dataset is only fetched once per year.

Created on 28.10.2026 @author: Mathias Berg Rosendal

PostDoc at DTU Management (Energy Economics & Modelling)

pybalmorel.backcasting.eurostat.decode_jsonstat(response: dict) → pandas.DataFrame

Decode a JSON-stat 2.0 dataset into one row per cell with a value.

Eurostat’s ‘value’ is sparse: a dict from the flat, row-major index over the dimensions in ‘id’ to the value. Cells without a value are left out.

Args:

response (dict): The parsed JSON-stat response.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: One column per dimension (holding its category codes)

and ‘Value’.
