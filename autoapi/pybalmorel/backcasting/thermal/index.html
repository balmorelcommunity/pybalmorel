pybalmorel.backcasting.thermal

Thermal Availability

Turn ENTSO-E’s installed generation capacity and outage reports into weekly GKRATE overrides (GKRATE_THERMAL_ENTSOE.inc) for the fossil, biomass and waste technologies of a Backcast: per canonical region, fuel group and Backcast Calendar week, the installed capacity as a share of GKFX (at most 1) less the share of it reported out of service (see CONTEXT.md). Observed output is never an input here, since the fossil mix is a Validation Metric; it only serves as a check in thermal_capacity.csv.

Also defines the fuel groups and the technology selection by acronym name that the must-run floor shares.

Created on 28.10.2026 @author: Mathias Berg Rosendal

PostDoc at DTU Management (Energy Economics & Modelling)

pybalmorel.backcasting.thermal.acronym_names(gdx_path: str | pathlib.Path, system_directory: str | None = None) → dict[float, str]

The names of the GAMS acronyms in a GDX file, keyed by the value GAMS stores for them.

A GAMS acronym is stored as its index times ACRONYM_SCALE, so e.g. GCND with index 224 is 2.24e303 in GDATA. Look values up with acronym_name.

Args:

gdx_path (str | Path): The GDX file, e.g. <scenario>_input_data.gdx. system_directory (str | None, optional): GAMS system directory, whose

GDX library is used. Defaults to None.

Returns:

dict[float, str]: Acronym name per stored value.

pybalmorel.backcasting.thermal.group_capacity(gkfx: pandas.DataFrame, gdata: pandas.DataFrame, rrraaa: pandas.DataFrame, names: dict[float, str], year: int, check_only: bool = False) → pandas.DataFrame

The GKFX of year per area and technology of every fuel group, with its canonical region.

Technologies are selected by the names of their GDTYPE (THERMAL_TYPES) and GDFUEL acronyms. Columns are taken by position, as get_input may name them after the raw GAMS domains.

Args:

gkfx (pd.DataFrame): GKFX from get_input, ending in (Y, AAA, G, Value). gdata (pd.DataFrame): GDATA from get_input, ending in (G, GDATASET, Value). rrraaa (pd.DataFrame): RRRAAA from get_input, ending in (RRR, AAA). names (dict[float, str]): Output of acronym_names. year (int): The Backcast Year, the Y of GKFX. check_only (bool, optional): Also include CHECK_ONLY_GROUPS (solar

and wind). Defaults to False.

Raises:
ValueError: If a thermal technology with GKFX > 0 has a fuel that is

in no group and not in UNGROUPED_FUELS.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Columns ‘Area’, ‘Technology’, ‘BalmorelRegion’,

‘Region’ (canonical), ‘Fuel’, ‘Group’ and ‘Capacity’ (MW), for every grouped (area, technology) with GKFX > 0.

The outage periods of the latest revision of every event, without cancelled or withdrawn events.

Exact duplicate rows are dropped first: ENTSO-E’s download repeats some periods of the same event and revision (10,495 of 69,492 rows in 2023), which would otherwise count those outages twice.

Args:

pybalmorel.backcasting.thermal.thermal_ratings(capacity: pandas.DataFrame, installed: pandas.Series, outage_energy: pandas.DataFrame | None) → pandas.DataFrame

The weekly Thermal Availability per canonical region and group.

k = min(1, installed / GKFX); u = energy out of service in the week / (installed x 168); rating = k (1 - u), clipped to [0, 1]. A group without an installed capacity gets no rating. Without outage reports for a region, u is 0.

Args:

capacity (pd.DataFrame): Output of group_capacity. installed (pd.Series): Output of group_installed for GROUP_PRODUCTION_TYPES. outage_energy (pd.DataFrame | None): Output of weekly_outage_energy,

or None if there are no outage reports at all.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Columns ‘Region’, ‘Group’, ‘Season’, ‘GKFX’, ‘Installed’,

‘k’, ‘Planned’, ‘Forced’ (u of each kind) and ‘Rating’.
