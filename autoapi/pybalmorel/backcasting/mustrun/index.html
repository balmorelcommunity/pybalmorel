pybalmorel.backcasting.mustrun

Must-run Floor

Turn ENTSO-E’s observed generation and day-ahead prices into the data of Balmorel’s MUSTRUN addon: per canonical region, fuel group and Backcast Calendar week, the lowest hourly output the group kept in a week where it would have paid to stop (see CONTEXT.md). Writes MUSTRUN_ZONE.inc, MUSTRUN_ZONE_RRR.inc, MUSTRUN_GROUP.inc, MUSTRUN_GROUP_FFF.inc and, last, MUSTRUN_FLOOR.inc, whose existence switches the addon on.

pybalmorel.backcasting.mustrun.must_run_floors(output: pandas.DataFrame, prices: pandas.DataFrame, year: int, price_threshold: float = PRICE_THRESHOLD, min_hours: int = MIN_HOURS, every_week: tuple[str, ...] = EVERY_WEEK_GROUPS) → pandas.DataFrame

The observed must-run floor per canonical region, fuel group and Backcast Calendar week.

The floor is the week’s lowest hourly output over all its hours, for every-week groups and for weeks with at least min_hours hours priced at or below price_threshold. A week with less than half its hours of output data gets no floor, nor does a floor below MIN_FLOOR.

Args:

output (pd.DataFrame): Output of group_output. prices (pd.DataFrame): Output of region_prices. year (int): The Backcast Year. price_threshold (float, optional): Nominal €/MWh. Defaults to PRICE_THRESHOLD. min_hours (int, optional): Low-price hours a week needs. Defaults to MIN_HOURS. every_week (tuple[str, …], optional): Groups floored every week.

Defaults to EVERY_WEEK_GROUPS.

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Columns ‘Region’, ‘Group’, ‘Season’, ‘Floor’ (MW, NaN

without one), ‘LowPriceHours’ and ‘ObservedTWh’, one row per region, group and week with output data.

pybalmorel.backcasting.mustrun.available_capacity(capacity: pandas.DataFrame, gkrate: pandas.DataFrame) → pandas.DataFrame

Available capacity K = sum of GKFX x GKRATE per canonical region, group and week.

Where GKRATE is not set, the rating is 1. A stored 0 is GAMS eps, since a GDX file doesn’t store zeros, so it counts as 0.

Args:

capacity (pd.DataFrame): Output of thermal.group_capacity. gkrate (pd.DataFrame): GKRATE from get_input, ending in (AAA, G, SSS, Value).

Returns:

pd.DataFrame: Columns ‘Region’, ‘Group’, ‘Season’ and ‘K’ (MW).

pybalmorel.backcasting.mustrun.cap_floors(floors: pandas.DataFrame, capacity: pandas.DataFrame, gkrate: pandas.DataFrame, balmorel_regions: list[str]) → pandas.DataFrame

Cap the floors at Balmorel’s available capacity, and drop those Balmorel can’t meet at all.

Floors of a canonical region without a Balmorel region are dropped. A group without GKFX in a region gets no floor and a warning; a floor above K is capped and warns.

Args:

floors (pd.DataFrame): Output of must_run_floors. capacity (pd.DataFrame): Output of thermal.group_capacity. gkrate (pd.DataFrame): GKRATE from get_input, ending in (AAA, G, SSS, Value). balmorel_regions (list[str]): The scenario’s RRR.

Returns:

pd.DataFrame: floors of the modelled regions, with ‘K’ (MW) and ‘Capped’ added.

pybalmorel.backcasting.mustrun.generate_must_run_floor(balmorel_model_folder: str | pathlib.Path, scenario: str, year: int, generation: dict[str, pandas.DataFrame], prices: pandas.DataFrame, load: pandas.DataFrame, price_threshold: float = PRICE_THRESHOLD, min_hours: int = MIN_HOURS, every_week: tuple[str, ...] = EVERY_WEEK_GROUPS, gams_system_directory: str | None = None, reload_input: bool = False) → list[pybalmorel.classes.IncFile]

Write the MUSTRUN files and backcastoutput/must_run_floor.csv for a scenario.

Args:

balmorel_model_folder (str | Path): Top-level Balmorel folder (contains ‘base’). scenario (str): The scenario to write the floor for. year (int): The Backcast Year. generation (dict[str, pd.DataFrame]): Hourly ENTSO-E output per

canonical region and production type.

prices (pd.DataFrame): Hourly day-ahead price per zone. load (pd.DataFrame): Hourly load per zone, to weight the prices of a region’s zones. price_threshold (float, optional): Nominal €/MWh. Defaults to PRICE_THRESHOLD. min_hours (int, optional): Low-price hours a week needs. Defaults to MIN_HOURS. every_week (tuple[str, …], optional): Groups floored every week.

Defaults to EVERY_WEEK_GROUPS.

gams_system_directory (str | None, optional): GAMS system directory. reload_input (bool, optional): Run ReadData again instead of reusing

the cached input GDX. Defaults to False.

Raises:

ValueError: If an every-week group isn’t a fuel group.

Returns:

list[IncFile]: The saved IncFile instances; empty if no floor qualifies.
