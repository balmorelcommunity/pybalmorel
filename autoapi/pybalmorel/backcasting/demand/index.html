pybalmorel.backcasting.demand

Observed Demand Profiles

Turn ENTSO-E’s actual load into DE_VAR_T overrides (DE_VAR_T_ENTSOE.inc) for a Backcast: the hourly load of the Backcast Year on the Backcast Calendar, for the exogenous electricity users OTHER and RESE of every region. Balmorel scales DE_VAR_T to the annual DE, so only the shape matters. Reuses the override-file pattern of docs/adr/0006 and appends the $include to the scenario’s DE_VAR_T.inc itself.

Implausible or missing hours are interpolated. A region with too many of them takes its profile from the input data instead, with the same cleaning, or keeps it unchanged if that fails too.

Also sets the annual level of that demand, the Demand Level (DE_ENTSOE.inc): ENTSO-E’s annual load less what the previous backcast run added on top of DE (its transmission losses, electricity for heat, and distribution losses on anything but exogenous demand), net of Balmorel’s distribution losses (heat=’model’, docs/adr/0015). That heating responds to prices, so the level is iterated over runs. The first run of a new Backcast Year takes Eurostat’s electricity for heating instead (heat=’eurostat’).

pybalmorel.backcasting.demand.demand_profiles(regions: list[str], observed: pandas.DataFrame, current: pandas.DataFrame, year: int) → tuple[dict[str, pandas.Series], list[str]]

The observed demand profile per Balmorel region, with a report line per region.

A region takes the ENTSO-E load of its canonical region, so DE4-* take DE’s. If that is missing or has more than MAX_IMPLAUSIBLE_SHARE implausible or missing hours, it takes its current profile from the input data instead, with the same cleaning. A region where both fail gets no profile and keeps its current one unchanged, which is warned about.

Args:

regions (list[str]): Balmorel regions with electricity demand. observed (pd.DataFrame): Hourly ENTSO-E load (MW) per canonical region,

indexed by timezone-aware timestamps (output of sum_zones).

current (pd.DataFrame): Output of current_profiles. year (int): The Backcast Year.

Returns:
tuple[dict[str, pd.Series], list[str]]: Profile in (S,T) order per

region, and one report line per region.

pybalmorel.backcasting.demand.annual_load(observed: pandas.DataFrame, year: int) → pandas.Series

ENTSO-E’s load per canonical region over Balmorel’s year (MWh).

The mean hourly load over the Backcast Calendar times Balmorel’s 8760 hours, so that a DE built on it gives back the observed load in the calendar’s 8736 hours, and is on the same annual footing as Eurostat’s heating and final consumption. Missing hours are filled with the mean, so a gap doesn’t lower the level.

Args:

observed (pd.DataFrame): Hourly load (MW) per canonical region (output of sum_zones). year (int): The Backcast Year.

Returns:

pd.Series: Index canonical region. A region without any hour is NaN.

pybalmorel.backcasting.demand.demand_levels(de: pandas.DataFrame, disloss: pandas.DataFrame, load: pandas.Series, balances: pandas.DataFrame, year: int, basis: str = 'entsoe', coverage_bounds: tuple[float, float] = COVERAGE_BOUNDS, heat: str = 'eurostat', previous: pandas.DataFrame | None = None) → tuple[pandas.DataFrame, pandas.DataFrame]

The Demand Level of year per region and user, with a summary per country.

With heat=’eurostat’, per country c, with L its ENTSO-E load, d its load-weighted DISLOSS_E and H its Eurostat electricity for heating, the level is L(1-d) - H (‘entsoe’) or FC_E - H (‘eurostat’). A country with several canonical regions (NO1-5) gets L_r(1-d_r) - H L_r/L per region (‘entsoe’) or its level split by L_r(1-d_r) (‘eurostat’).

With heat=’model’ (basis ‘entsoe’ only), per canonical region k, with T_k, E_k and O_k the previous run’s transmission losses, electricity for heat (ENDOGENOUS_ELECT2HEAT) and distribution losses on anything but exogenous demand (DIST_LOSSES less EXO d_r/(1-d_r) per region r: on electric heating, and on wind and solar output through DISLOSS_E_AG), summed over its Balmorel regions and scaled from the calendar’s 8736 hours to Balmorel’s 8760, the level is (L_k - T_k - E_k - O_k)(1 - d_k). Balmorel’s load as compared, EXO + E2H + DIST_LOSSES + TRANS_LOSSES, is then ENTSO-E’s if the run’s heating and losses stay as they were, so the level is iterated.

Within a canonical region (DE4-*) and then over the users, the level is split by the shares of de, so a rerun on its own output keeps them. A country is not written, and warns, without ENTSO-E load, if L(1-d)/FC_E is outside coverage_bounds, if a level is not positive, and with ‘eurostat’ heat without Eurostat final consumption or heating, or with ‘model’ heat if a region is not in the previous run. With ‘model’ heat, a country without Eurostat final consumption (CH) is written with its coverage unchecked.

Args:

de (pd.DataFrame): DE from get_input, ending in (YYY, RRR, DEUSER, Value). disloss (pd.DataFrame): DISLOSS_E from get_input, ending in (RRR, Value).

A region without one has no losses.

load (pd.Series): Output of split_shared_load (MWh per canonical region or country). balances (pd.DataFrame): Output of eurostat.electricity_balances (GWh). year (int): The Backcast Year. basis (str, optional): ‘entsoe’ or ‘eurostat’. Defaults to ‘entsoe’. coverage_bounds (tuple[float, float], optional): Lowest and highest

L(1-d)/FC_E written. Defaults to COVERAGE_BOUNDS.

heat (str, optional): ‘eurostat’ or ‘model’, where the heating left

out comes from. Defaults to ‘eurostat’.

previous (pd.DataFrame | None, optional): Output of previous_run_heat.

Needed for ‘model’ heat; with ‘eurostat’ heat it only fills the previous-run columns of the summary.

Returns:
tuple[pd.DataFrame, pd.DataFrame]: The level per ‘Region’ and ‘User’

(‘Value’, MWh) of the countries written, and the summary per country (TWh). ‘InputTWh’ is the input data’s DE, which after a first run is the previous level. ‘ResidualTWh’ is the model-heat level less the previous run’s exogenous load: what that run’s load missed ENTSO-E’s by, net of distribution losses.

Warn where the previous run’s exogenous load isn’t the Demand Level it should have solved with.

pybalmorel.backcasting.demand.check_de_wrappers(data_path: str | pathlib.Path, base_data_path: str | pathlib.Path) → None

Warn if a DE addon of base runs without the scenario re-applying DE_ENTSOE.inc after it.

The INDUSTRY and INDIVUSERS addons adjust DE after DE.inc, so the scenario needs its own INDUSTRY_DE.inc and INDIVUSERS_DE.inc that include base’s and then DE_ENTSOE.inc again. Otherwise Balmorel solves with another DE than the Demand Level.

Write DE_ENTSOE.inc and backcastoutput/demand_level.csv for a Balmorel scenario.

The scenario’s DE.inc must include DE_ENTSOE.inc after base’s DE.inc, and its INDUSTRY_DE.inc and INDIVUSERS_DE.inc again after base’s. With ‘model’ heat, the heating and transmission losses come from the previous run’s balmorel_load.csv, which must be the run that solved with the scenario’s current DE_ENTSOE.inc (warned about if not). Its residual is printed, for the user to decide whether to iterate again.

Args:

balmorel_model_folder (str | Path): Top-level Balmorel folder (contains ‘base’). scenario (str): The scenario to write the override for. year (int): The Backcast Year. zones (pd.DataFrame): Hourly ENTSO-E load (MW) per bidding zone,

indexed by timezone-aware timestamps.

eurostat_data_path (str | Path | None, optional): Cache folder of the

Eurostat responses. Defaults to <scenario>/eurostatdata.

basis (str, optional): ‘entsoe’ or ‘eurostat’. Defaults to ‘entsoe’. coverage_bounds (tuple[float, float], optional): Lowest and highest

L(1-d)/FC_E written. Defaults to COVERAGE_BOUNDS.

gams_system_directory (str | None, optional): GAMS system directory. reload_input (bool, optional): Run ReadData again instead of reusing

the cached input GDX. Defaults to False.

heat (str, optional): ‘model’ takes the heating from the previous

run, ‘eurostat’ from Eurostat (the first run of a new Backcast Year). Defaults to ‘model’.

load_results (str | Path | None, optional): The previous run’s

balmorel_load.csv. Defaults to <scenario>/backcastoutput/balmorel_load.csv.

Raises:

FileNotFoundError: With ‘model’ heat, if there is no previous run.

Returns:

IncFile: The saved IncFile instance.
