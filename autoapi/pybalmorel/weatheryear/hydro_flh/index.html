pybalmorel.weatheryear.hydro_flh

Absolute Hydro FLH

Hydro full-load hours as CorRES inflow energy divided by the Balmorel capacity that receives it (hydro_flh: mode: absolute), instead of a factor on Balmorel’s base FLH. Run-of-river energy above the run-of-river capacity moves to the same area’s reservoir, and countries whose CorRES inflow fails a plausibility check, or that CorRES covers only partly or not at all, get the Hydro Inflow Fallback (see CONTEXT.md, ADR 0011).

Everything but load_balmorel_hydro_inputs works on DataFrames, so it can be tested without GAMS.

pybalmorel.weatheryear.hydro_flh.move_spilled_run_of_river(res: pandas.DataFrame, ror: pandas.DataFrame, capacity: pandas.DataFrame) → tuple[pandas.DataFrame, pandas.DataFrame]

The yearly energies after moving run-of-river energy Balmorel can’t take to the reservoir.

Per year and area, the spill is the run-of-river inflow above the run-of-river capacity, summed over the hours (all of it without run-of-river capacity). With reservoir capacity in the area it moves to the reservoir; without, it stays and is reported.

Args:

res (pd.DataFrame): Hourly reservoir inflow (MW) per area. ror (pd.DataFrame): Hourly run-of-river inflow (MW) per area. capacity (pd.DataFrame): Output of hydro_capacity.

Returns:
tuple[pd.DataFrame, pd.DataFrame]: Yearly reservoir and run-of-river

energy (MWh), index year, one column per area.

pybalmorel.weatheryear.hydro_flh.plausibility_failures(res: pandas.DataFrame, ror: pandas.DataFrame, capacity: pandas.DataFrame, countries: pandas.Series, reference: dict[str, float]) → dict[str, str]

The countries whose CorRES inflow fails the plausibility check, with the reason.

A country fails if a reservoir column equals the run-of-river column of the same area, if a column equals another area’s column in either file, if its long-term CorRES energy is more than PLAUSIBLE_DEVIATION from the reference, or if CorRES covers its hydro capacity only partly or not at all. A country missing from the reference can’t be checked on its level and is reported.

Args:

res (pd.DataFrame): Hourly reservoir inflow (MW) per area. ror (pd.DataFrame): Hourly run-of-river inflow (MW) per area. capacity (pd.DataFrame): Output of hydro_capacity. countries (pd.Series): Output of area_countries. reference (dict[str, float]): Long-term Natural-Inflow Hydro per country (TWh/yr).

Returns:

dict[str, str]: Failing country -> reason.

pybalmorel.weatheryear.hydro_flh.fallback_flh(country: str, capacity: pandas.DataFrame, countries: pandas.Series, flh_base: pandas.DataFrame, factors: dict[str, pandas.Series], missing_factor: float, reference: dict[str, float]) → pandas.DataFrame

The Hydro Inflow Fallback FLH for every hydro area and type of a country.

FLH_a,t = E_c x f_a,t x FLHbase_a,t / sum over the country of (FLHbase x K), with E_c the reference long-term energy and f the relative factor of the year: the pair’s own, or the country’s for a pair without a CorRES column (1 in the long-term files). A missing FLHbase counts as 0, so that pair gets no line. A country without a reference value gets FLHbase x f.

Args:

country (str): The country. capacity (pd.DataFrame): Output of hydro_capacity. countries (pd.Series): Output of area_countries. flh_base (pd.DataFrame): Output of base_flh. factors (dict[str, pd.Series]): The relative factor per area with a

CorRES column, for each symbol.

missing_factor (float): The factor for pairs without a CorRES column. reference (dict[str, float]): Long-term Natural-Inflow Hydro per country (TWh/yr).

Returns:
pd.DataFrame: Index area, one column per symbol, NaN where the area

has no capacity or base FLH of that type.
