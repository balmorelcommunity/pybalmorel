pybalmorel.weatheryear.hydro_reference

Hydro Reference

Observed long-term Natural-Inflow Hydro per country, the reference for the plausibility check and the Hydro Inflow Fallback of Absolute Hydro FLH (see CONTEXT.md and docs/adr/0011). Bundled, like the deflator table of ADR 0009, so a weather-year run needs no download.

Mean annual gross generation 2013-2022 (the ten years before the newest weather year in the 2023 inputs, never the Backcast Year itself), TWh/yr, to 0.1 GWh.

Sources, retrieved 2026-10-22: - Eurostat nrg_bal_c, nrg_bal=PPRD, siec=RA100, unit=GWH (primary production

of hydro). Pumped-storage output, including the pumped part of mixed plants, is booked separately under RA130 (Energy Balance Guide), so RA100 is natural inflow only. nrg_bal_peh has no RA110/RA120 split; its GEP RA100 equals PPRD RA100 except for UK and UA, where it includes pumped output. Eurostat ‘EL’ is ‘GR’ here. UK covers 2013-2019, UA 2013-2020 and BA 2014-2022; every other country 2013-2022.

  • CH (not in Eurostat): Swiss Federal Office of Energy, electricity balance (opendata ogd32), run-of-river + storage generation minus 0.73 x storage pump consumption - Eurostat’s default for pumped output when it isn’t measured. Calendar years.
