pybalmorel.weatheryear.additional_inc

Functions for generating additional Balmorel .inc files from CorRES VRE data.

Creates generator sets (GGG, AAA, RRRAAA), investment data (INVDATA, INVDATASET, ALLOWEDINV, ANNUITYCG), potential tables (SUBTECHGROUPKPOT), capacity factors (GKFX) and GDATA for renewable technologies.

@author: Polyneikis Kanellas, Development Engineer DTU Wind
